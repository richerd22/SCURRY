# SCURRY - Player FAQ

**What is this?**
A first-person squirrel game on a full model of Boston Common + the Public
Garden. No missions, no shooting, no winning screen - you are a squirrel,
winter is coming, and the only score that matters is how much food you
bury before you get there. Play it at https://scurry.cold.fyi - it runs
in the browser on desktop and phones, nothing to install.

**How do I play on my phone?**
Open the site and tap TAP TO PLAY. Left thumbstick moves, drag the right
side of the screen to look, and the round buttons are JUMP, BITE, ACT
(beg / take / interact) and BURY, plus a SPRINT toggle. It works in
Safari and in in-app browsers (the ones inside Instagram, etc.) - no
pointer lock or special mode needed.

**How do I get food?**
- **Beg:** walk up to someone sitting on a bench and press E / ACT. Look
  cute. Receive snacks.
- **Steal:** picnics have food on the blanket. The owners look away -
  sometimes. If they're looking at the blanket, wait or accept the risk.
- **Trash cans:** undignified. Jackpots.
- **The pretzel cart** by the bandstand: the vendor can be begged too.

**What do I do with food?**
Carry one item at a time. Press F / BURY on grass to bury it: stash +1
and your winter meter fills. Eating is not simulated. This is a hoarding
game. We respect it.

**The dogs?**
Off-leash and extremely interested in you. On the ground they'll chase;
they can't climb. If one catches you it just knocks your food loose and
boops you - you're fine, it's humiliating. BITE (click / BITE button) at
close range boops the nose right back and the dog leaves to reconsider
its choices.

**The ranger?**
He patrols the paths with a net and a deep personal commitment to the
rules. He chases squirrels he catches on the ground near him, or any
squirrel he's seen stealing. Bite his ankle to end a chase. If he nets
you, you wake up on the bandstand roof and he takes 25% of your stash as
a fine. Relocated, never harmed. Nobody dies in this game. The geese are
coming in a later update, and they will be worse.

**What's the Hollow?**
There's a great tree by the Frog Pond with a knot-hole at squirrel
height. Slip through it (E / ACT). Inside: Elder Bramble, who knows
everything that happens in the park and will tell you, and your vault -
the pile physically grows with your stash.

**Can I climb trees?**
Yes - push into any trunk and keep pushing. Canopy tops are walkable.
Leap tree to tree. There is no fall damage. Squirrels do not take fall
damage; this is science.

**Does the time of day change?**
A full day runs about 13 minutes: morning, midday, a long golden hour,
dusk, night (lamps on, windows lit, stars out), dawn. The park is best at
golden hour. The squirrel has opinions about this and so do we.

**Is my progress saved?**
Not yet. The stash resets when you close the tab. Saving, the canopy
highway network, burrow tunnels, the hawk (her name is Hawk Dua), geese,
crows, and multiplayer are all on the roadmap.

**Who made this?**
Third Polar Arch. The dogs, humans and lamp posts are CC0 models by
Quaternius and Kay Lousberg; everything else is procedural - code-built
landmarks, painted-canvas textures, synthesized sound. No AI-generated
imagery.
